Why Willowbrook?

Qualified Teachers

All staff are certified specialists with ongoing professional development.

Outdoor Learning

Forest school, gardening, and nature trails integrated into curriculum.

Wellbeing Focus

Daily mindfulness, school counselor, and anti-bullying programs.

Digital Literacy

Modern computer labs, coding from Year 2, safe internet practices.

Our Mission

To empower every child with knowledge, resilience, and kindness in a safe, vibrant environment that celebrates diversity and sparks lifelong curiosity.

Our Vision

To be a beacon of inclusive primary education where each student thrives academically, socially, and emotionally, ready to shape a brighter future.

Core Values

Respect • Responsibility • Integrity • Compassion • Perseverance
